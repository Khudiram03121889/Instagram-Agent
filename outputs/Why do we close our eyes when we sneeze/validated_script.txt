Title: Why Eyes Close on Sneezes

Full Script:
Ever noticed your eyes close when you sneeze? That eye shut is a fast brainstem reflex you cannot control. The brainstem signal twitch spreads, triggering eyes to shut in one muscle burst. Closing your eyes protects them from the sneeze pressure wave automatically. This automatic protection reflex package keeps you safe.
