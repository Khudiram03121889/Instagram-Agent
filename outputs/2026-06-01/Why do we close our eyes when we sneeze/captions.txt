Ever tried keeping your eyes open during a sneeze? Spoiler: You can't! 🤧❌

Here is the mind-blowing science behind why your body automatically hits the "close" switch on your eyelids:

🧠 The Brainstem Command: When a sneeze is triggered, your brain's sneezing center sends a coordinated motor command down your nervous system.
🫁 Coordinated Contraction: This single signal makes your chest, throat, and eyelids contract together in one rapid, involuntary movement.
🛡️ Biological Shield: Closing your eyes protects your delicate ocular surfaces from the high-pressure blast of air, dust, and micro-particles escaping your nose!

Isn't it fascinating how our body's reflexes work as a built-in defense shield? 🧬✨
